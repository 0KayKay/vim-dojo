# Playtest 3

## General Remarks

- I find strange that enter on a finished challenge leads to the details of the challenge instead of the next level. Inspecting details should get a different button.
- The single levels or rather their challenges could be a bit longer. A minimum of 10 rounds, maybe rather 20. And bossfights maybe more like 30 rounds, separated into 3 waves of 10 rounds each. Then we can do an S curve per wave of how hard a round is or how many chains of movements it demands in a row it takes. And that S curve gets steeper for each of the three waves.
- It would be good to have a "lives" indicator at the top of the screen of how many rounds you can lose before you fail to advance in challenges and bosses.
- Maybe we should note down a convention for all of the different elements of the game...

## World 1

- 1.1 and 1.2 feel snappy
- 1.3 has some problems. I like the inline text, but it's impossible to see under the cursor or on a blank space. It's tough to get right quickly because you have to guess whether to input another character or not. And if it's under the cursor, that should get a visual representation, because it otherwise requires losing moves to check.
- 1.4 has again the whitespace problem. I'm thinking about underlining the green text in a similar fashin as text to be deleted is strikethrough. That could be hard to differentiate from underscores, but they are a lot less common than whitespaces from what I recall in these levels.
- Time limits for the regular levels are trivial until now. But maybe we could add intervals for the used time that give different ammounts of points. Also it would be nice if there was a visual indicator in the UI that changes every time you drop a tier. So I'd also do three stars for that: three for below the toughest threshold, two and one for a bit longer, and none for a timeout. That way you don't get frustrated because you can't output the last character you were planning, but you still have an incentive to try being your fastest. You can orient yourself on my best times for the three stars for now until we get some crazy kids to try this.
- Okay, time limits for the bossfights are waaay to easy. These three seconds per new chain are completely over the top. One is enough, or whatever variable you're using per keystroke. 


## World 2

- 2.1 had the first disappointment for me. It was like "aww man, I don't get to use my new move" because there are so many instances of items being waaay too close to warrant using numbers. For subsequent levels it's fine if that's the case, but for this specific challenge I think it's important that we *have* to use numbers.
- 2.2 has a similar problem because the sideways movement is often so close that you can simply use 'h' and 'l' to get there in a reasonable time instead of words. It's fine if it's one to three words away, but for one to three charakters it's a bit lame.
- 2.3 feels snappier again for some reason. 
- For all of the word-based levels it would be a good idea to add templates (again, common words would help) for rounds that have you enter a prefix or a postfix to the start or the end of a word, maybe a comma or a few letters. That's a common use case to move to the start or the end of a word and then add something with i or a.
- 2.4 is fine as is. In this case it doesn't make as much sense as in the last two levels to directly do an edit, because you would rather use 'A' or 'I' for that to save a keystroke. Maybe this one is more relevant for deleting things. We can just 'x' a few characters for now, but later this could be a candidate for 'dw' or something.j
- Boss: I was kind of confused to see rounds where there were multiple characters to remove at the end of a word. I'd personally go with insert plus backspace there because I find it weird to move to the end of the word and then away from there to a precise location instead of doing the cognitively easier thing and just either completely 'cw'ing and retyping the thing or going to the end and backspacing before typing something new. We're still trying to establish  practical habits.
- Again, time limits for the boss are trivial. I tried once how long I had to wait after i royally stumbled, and waited with my last input until the time limit kicked. It was ages. Those three seconds per new chain absolutely kill the utility of it.
 
## World 3

- 3.1 the time limit actually got me once or twice in the beginning, after I mashed my fingers. It's really better suited for things like this. Or I'm already beginning to see the speed up of things I do all the time, and the elements of the levels before really show up all the time. It feels fair though. I like the use of delete to the end of word, but from a point of the cursor that is not the first character. Or something like 'd3b'. It's creative challenge contents that show up in real life.
- 3.2 is similarly good
- I find that both 3.2 and 3.3 could use something like d^ or c^ backwards from the cursor. Especially relevant in programming. That's a whopper to start a world if you encounter all of that combined for the first time, but if the habits of the worlds before are already there, it should be fine. I hope.
- 3.3 is pretty chill. I also think having higher numbers for something like this is fine, since you can see the exact number on the side.
- Boss: I noticed only when I tackled the boss that I didn't consciously encounter a level before that uses 'b' wrapping over into the last line. I found that when looking for the optimal solution in the "post mortem" menu of the boss challenge. Either I didn't try to optimize that when movement across words was introduced or it wasn't there... Anyway, maximizing points for this boss is definitely the most cognitively challenging. However, it gets *easier* on multi turn rounds because of the tons of time added in between.
- I feel like challenges and bosses from this world shouldn't need to do raw movements that are not combined with anything, where there's just a location marked. The edits contain all the movements you'd want. I mean, it's mostly the stuff that is combined with movements, so of course there's a ton. I also think that after this world you shouldn't get any raw movements than the ones you just learned anymore, otherwise only movements that are combined with stuff.
