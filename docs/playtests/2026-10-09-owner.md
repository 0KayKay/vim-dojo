## Remarks given in chat (summarized)

- World 1, and its boss above all, feels much harder than Worlds 2 and 3
  because it asks for an unnatural way to move: sideways with counts on `h` and
  `l`. Nobody estimates a distance in letters and counts `l` presses by eye;
  you would use `w`, `b`, `e`, `f` or `t`, or wipe the word with `cw` and
  retype it. Proposal: move counts out of World 1 to the start of World 2, and
  limit World 1 to two, three, at most four repeated presses.
- Plucking letters out of the middle of a word with `4x` is just as unnatural;
  stray letters should sit near the cursor, ideally at a word's end.
- `i` and `a` are a good way to teach changes in the proximity, for example
  saving an `l` by pressing `a` at the right moment.
- The clock is a bit tight; 0.8 s per key instead of 0.7 s. As a casual user,
  finishing the last keystroke just as the timeout shows is frustrating,
  especially in multi-step boss rounds.
- The git-diff style markup is confusing and feels like a bug.
- The `<Tab>` hint went unused for lack of time. More useful would be opening a
  round ("a room in a dungeon") from the results to look at its setup without
  replaying it, as a post-mortem.
- Before merging 0.5, fix the timing, the markup and the World 1 boss ("you
  cannot have a beginner do that"); call the result 0.6.
